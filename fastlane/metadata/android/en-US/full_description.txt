Camera Probe finds out what your phone's camera system lets a regular app use. Stock camera apps get special access to the hardware; Camera Probe shows what any camera app from a store can actually reach on your device, model and Android version.

What it checks
• Every camera, including the physical lenses behind each logical camera
• Hardware level, RAW, manual sensor and post-processing control, burst, 10-bit and stream use cases
• A processing tier for each camera, A, B or C, from what it supports
• Stream sizes and formats, zoom ranges, multi-resolution and depth outputs
• Every metadata key and the vendor tags the manufacturer adds
• Camera2 extensions such as Night, HDR and Bokeh
• Shared and native libraries and accelerators your phone's software makes available to apps
• Burst, JPEG, reprocessing and vendor capture tests, with timings

How it works
• Choose the probes and tap Run; the ones that open the camera say so
• Follow the log as it runs, then read a summary of every camera
• Export the results as a zip to any folder, or share it with any app
• Test photos never go into the zip
• Built-in profiles for Google, Qualcomm, MediaTek, Samsung, Unisoc, OPPO/OnePlus, vivo, Xiaomi, ASUS, Motorola, Sony and Huawei/Honor phones

Useful for camera app developers, custom ROM makers and anyone curious about their phone.

Private and open
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• No internet permission: results leave your phone only when you export or share them
• Only the camera permission
• Free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: github.com/PVOT-OSS/CameraProbe
